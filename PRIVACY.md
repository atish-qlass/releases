# Privacy policy - Class Monitor and its Chrome extension

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Class Monitor is used by QLASS Edtech on its own teaching computers. Its Chrome extension, "Class Monitor - Meet Bridge", runs only on https://meet.google.com.

**What it reads.** While a Google Meet call is open, the extension reads the meeting title, whether the microphone is muted, whether the screen is being presented, and the names of people waiting to join.

**Where it goes.** This information is sent only to the Class Monitor desktop app on the same computer, over the local address 127.0.0.1. It is not sent to the internet, to QLASS servers, to Google, or to anyone else.

**What it stores.** The extension stores nothing. The desktop app keeps a local log on that computer of meeting times and period changes (no recordings, no guest names).

**What it does on your behalf.** When the desktop app asks, the extension clicks buttons in Meet that the teacher could click: Admit, Join, microphone, camera and Present.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared or used for advertising or any purpose other than the above.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/atish-qlass/releases/issues.
